About

From benchmark scores to operational utility

Earth observation has moved from task-specific models to general-purpose pretrained representations such as Google's Satellite Embeddings and TESSERA. This workshop puts the people who build them in the same room as the remote sensing and Earth science researchers who have to rely on them.

Scale.Can one embedding drive continental mapping without region-by-region retraining?
Scarcity.Do foundation models really cut the label budget in underrepresented geographies?

Call for papers

What to submit, and how

Original papers that evaluate, adapt, or deploy foundation and embedding models for real-world Earth observation. Negative results and application papers from Earth science groups are explicitly welcome.

  1. Write it in the official ICVGIP 2026 template, anonymized.
  2. Submit on OpenReview (link posted when the call opens).
  3. Reviewed by computer vision and Earth observation experts.
  4. Accepted papers: short oral plus archival proceedings.
LengthUp to 8 pages, excluding references
TemplateOfficial ICVGIP 2026 paper template
ReviewDouble blind, multiple reviewers per paper
PlatformOpenReview (link to follow)
ProceedingsArchival, via the ICVGIP 2026 workshop proceedings
PresentationShort oral by at least one registered author

Challenge

Geospatial model-making challenge

Details announced with the call

Adapt any public foundation or embedding model to a geospatial task. Scoring is on held-out regions, so geographic generalization decides the leaderboard.

What to expect

  • A released training split and a spatially disjoint test split
  • Freedom to use any public pretrained backbone or embedding
  • A short technical report from each participating team
  • Top entries present at the workshop
